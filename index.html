The compliance problem

A single records request can become a lawsuit.

Florida law gives owners a broad right to inspect official records — and gives associations only a short window to respond. Miss it, or hand over a protected document by mistake, and the association faces statutory damages plus the owner's attorney's fees.

⏱

Tight statutory clocks

Condos must provide access within 10 working days; HOAs within 10 business days. Missing the window creates a rebuttable presumption of willful noncompliance.

⚠

Records you must NOT disclose

Attorney-client material, personnel and medical records, SSNs, and bank data are statutorily protected. Disclosing them by accident creates its own liability.

📋

New checklist duty (condos)

Since 2024, condominiums must give the requestor a checklist of records made available and withheld — and keep it for 7 years.
