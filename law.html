Official records & owner inspection rights

A plain-language walkthrough of what Florida's Condominium Act (Ch. 718) and Homeowners' Association Act (Ch. 720) require — the records associations must keep, how fast they must produce them, what they must withhold, and what happens when they get it wrong.

Chapter 720

Homeowners Association

DeadlineAccess within 10 business days of a written request § 720.303(5)(a)
LocationWithin 45 miles of the community or within the county
If you miss itRebuttable presumption of willful noncompliance
Penalty$50/day up to 10 days ($500 min.) + prevailing member's attorney's fees
Access floorMay not restrict inspection to less than one 8-hour business day per month
The right is broad. An association may adopt reasonable rules on the frequency, time, location, and manner of inspection — but it may not require the owner to state a reason for the request. When in doubt, the statutory default favors disclosure of anything not specifically protected.

§ 718.111(12) — 2024 amendment

The condominium records checklist

As of 2024, a condominium association responding to a records request must provide the requestor a checklist identifying the records that were made available and those that were withheld. That checklist must be retained for 7 years. It is both a compliance duty and, done right, the association's best evidence that it responded fully and lawfully.
