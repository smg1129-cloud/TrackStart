Owner records request portal

Request to inspect your association's records

You have a statutory right to inspect your association's official records. Submit a written request below and track it against the statutory deadline. You do not need to explain why you want the records.

Demo — your request is stored in this browser and appears in the association's client portal.

What happens next

1 · The association receives your written request.
2 · It must make records available within the statutory deadline.
3 · Protected information is redacted or withheld as the law requires.
4 · You receive the records and a checklist of what was provided and withheld.

Your rights, briefly

You may inspect and copy official records at your reasonable expense. The association may set reasonable inspection rules but cannot ask why you want the records. If it misses the deadline, the law presumes willful noncompliance. Learn more →
