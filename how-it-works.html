The workflow

From a filing cabinet to a compliant response.

RecordsGuard FL turns a scramble into a repeatable process: digitize once, let AI organize and flag, then fulfill every owner request with a documented, defensible trail.

1

Upload

Your team drags records into the secure client portal — PDFs, scans, spreadsheets, photos of paper files.

2

AI reads & files

Each document is read, classified into its statutory records category, and placed in the matching folder automatically.

3

Flag & review

Anything with SSNs, bank data, PHI, or contact info is held in a review queue for a human to redact and approve.

4

Fulfill

When an owner requests records, approved documents are released and a statutory checklist is generated.

Area 2 · Password-protected

A client area where records organize themselves

Behind a secure login, your association uploads its records and the AI component does the filing. It reads each document, decides which statutory category it belongs to, and drops it into the right folder — turning a banker's box into a structured, searchable archive.

Crucially, it also protects you. The same pass that classifies a document scans it for sensitive information — Social Security numbers, bank and account numbers, protected health information, and personal contact details — and flags it for human review before it can ever be released to an owner.

Area 3 · Owner-facing

A portal that fulfills the request — and proves it

Owners submit their inspection request through a dedicated portal and watch its status against the statutory clock. The association releases only approved, redacted records — and the system produces the records checklist that Chapter 718 now requires (and that's smart practice for HOAs too), documenting exactly what was provided and what was withheld, with the statutory basis for each.

A note on this prototype

What's real here, and what a production build adds

This demonstration runs entirely in your browser so you can experience the full workflow immediately. The AI classification and sensitive-data detection use on-device heuristics on the text you provide; uploaded files never leave your machine, and data is stored only in your browser.

Production version. A deployed system adds real accounts and encrypted authentication, server-side storage with per-association isolation, a document AI/OCR model that reads scanned pages, human-in-the-loop redaction tooling, immutable audit logs, and role-based access for board members, managers, and counsel.
